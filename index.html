Overview

A2A-UAV is acquired by a UAV-mounted platform equipped with an RGB–thermal gimbal camera, a panoramic camera, an RF spectrum analyzer and a millimeter-wave radar. Fifteen target UAVs of thirteen commercial models were recorded at three outdoor sites under daytime and nighttime conditions.

Each sample links the five observations of the same target through a common sample identifier. All bounding boxes were annotated manually and cross-checked.
